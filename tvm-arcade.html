HOW TO PLAY

GOAL: clear every stage of a chapter, each ending in a BOSS — a full case straight out of your notes (Ms. Varner's mutual fund, the Tuccis' RV, Sharon's college fund, Bill Brown's mortgage…).

Two chapters share the cabinet: CH.1 The Human Interface (communication) and M2 CH.1 Time Value Raiders (the money math). Each keeps its own progress, and the TVM calculator appears only where it's useful.

THE LEARNING PART

Every miss stops the clock and shows you the rule from the notes, then re-queues that question at the end of the wave — you don't leave a stage until you can answer it. Your accuracy per topic is tracked in STUDY DATA, and DRILL WEAK SPOTS feeds you only the ones you keep missing.

FREE PLAY

Endless mixed drills, no lives, no timer pressure — pure study mode.
