## 3. Major defects

1. **The traffic copies real cars.** In all-05 (left), the van has a Volkswagen Type 2 microbus outline: round roof, a high band of round-cornered windows, chrome hubcaps. The pink coupe's buttressed tail is close to a Ferrari 308/328 GTB. Fix: remodel the van as a flat-fronted cab-over, give the coupe a fastback tail, and check all traffic against real 1960s-90s cars.
2. **Boss Rush prints text over text on every hit.** In br-04 t8-14, "-19 HP" lands across "PRODUCT PUSHER Argh! You set…", and neither reads. The same happens in br-07 t1-12 and in t20 of each fight sheet; the question panel clips "LIGHTNING!" (br-12 t1-2). Fix: give popups their own lane beside the hit point, below the caption plate, and hold the caption until they clear.
3. **Risk Blaster's low-health state can't be seen.** rb-15 t1-12 has no red frame (the edges stay near black, R 5 to 16 and never above B), no flame and no smoke, and the engines sit below the band. Fix: use Boss Rush's beating frame (br-06-last-heart) round the band, and raise the camera or move the emitter onto the visible hull. Strengthen ne-13's frame too.
4. **The examiner's chest is a black smear.** In ui-13 and all-02 t9, a soft, shapeless blob fills the centre of the screen, in matte orange armour with no panel lines, under a mushy skull. Fix: a hard-edged recess (bevelled rim, vents or a glowing core), panel lines, varied roughness, a cool key and a magenta rim.
5. **Risk Blaster's ambient ships are lumps.** In all-07 the cruiser (top right) is a pink smear, and the freighter and tug are clay pods; in rb-15 t1-12 the freighter by the T drone reads as a cloud. Fix: hard-surface silhouettes (bridge, engines, lit ports, navigation lights), or make them smaller and sink them in haze.
6. **RANK UP! can't be read.** In ui-08 t12 the plate is see-through: a dim "RANK UP!" sits over ACCURACY 100%, and "You are now SENIOR ADVISOR" shows through the BEST STREAK and HIGH SCORE labels. Fix: an opaque plate with a border, and a scrim over the tiles.
7. **The UI piles up when the exam is passed.** In ui-14 t1 the trophy toast covers the question panel and the FINAL BOSS label, and NEXT and SKIP overlap ("NEX SKIP"). Fix: clear the question UI and NEXT before the banner and SKIP; hold the toast until the cinematic ends.

## 5. Originality

- **Lookalikes:** the VW-style microbus and the 308-style coupe (all-05, defect 1).
- **Archetypes, acceptable:** the Patrimony Phantom (a hooded reaper); the horned, bat-winged demon (all-11); the Probate Tollgate (a box on tracks, no binocular head, so not WALL-E); Lord Intestate (black caped armour, but the wig keeps it from Vader).
- **Original:** the starfighter, the egg-carrying rig, the other bosses, the cabinets and their names, and the INR ARCADE logo. The ne-11 fastback is a generic 1980s coupe.
