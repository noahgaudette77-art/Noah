# The finished game: the captures for the final review

Made on the final build, served over HTTP with every asset loaded, on a 390 x 844 phone screen at twice its density. Stills are whole screens or the 3D band of a game screen; strips are frames side by side, left to right then top to bottom (the spacing column says how far apart; at 60 frames a second, "2 frames apart" is 30 tiles a second). The questions and notes are the course's own study content: judge their legibility, not their wording.
