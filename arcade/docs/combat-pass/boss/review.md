## Major defects

1. **The speech box hides the boss.** 12-explanation-full (only its legs show), 06-last-heart t2-6, 10-player-defeat t4-10, *-death t1-10, 10-tollgate-fight t17-19 (over the chest as the lance lands). Fix: a one-line caption at the band's top edge; hide it while the explanation sheet is up and when the death starts.
2. **Win and loss go unshown.** No "BOSS DEFEATED!" in 09-death t14-18. "DEFEATED" shows in one tile only, behind the boss (10-player-defeat t7), and "GAME OVER" never shows. Fix: banners on the top layer, held for at least 1.5 s.
3. **Strikes never reach the player.** Slams end in sparks at the boss's feet (02/05/08-fight t12-16); waves stay as rings on its chest (03, 06-fight t13-16); no wave or seeker landing is shown. Fix: a floor shockwave to the bottom edge, a wave that grows to fill the band, a flash on landing.
4. **Attacks repeat.** Perilmonger, Golem and Intestate slam the same way, with the same triangle; Specter and Titan, same rings; Lich and Influencer, same chevrons and orbs; Fine Print and Phantom, same arc and beam; Pusher, Tollgate and Proctor throw the same lined card. Fix: a projectile, trail and impact for each boss.
5. **The last heart can't be seen.** The red frame is a dim maroon and doesn't change across 06-last-heart t2-6. Fix: a saturated red vignette that pulses about once a second.
6. **Product Pusher's face** (02-idle): a grinning chrome skull in dark glasses reads as a T-800 in shades. Fix: a faceplate or grille in place of the teeth.
7. **The undead look alike.** Specter, Lich and Phantom are all hooded or caped under the same red sky, and the Lich and the Phantom both show ribcages. Remake the Lich.
8. **The damage that lasts can't be read.** Big hits drop the boss behind the foreground heads (07-damage-states t7-8, t11-12); in 08-rage no wound shows. Fix: a shallower recoil; glowing edges and smoke on the wounds.
9. **Debris looks like a standing boss.** After the last blast, the cape and ribcage rise upright in mid-frame (11-phantom-death t14-15, 07-lich-death t14-15). Fix: shred or burn the capes; throw pieces down and out.
10. **Blocky sky.** The violet storm is stair-stepped and low-resolution in 01-entrance t1-6 and in the wide shots of 02/04/09-fight. Fix: a higher-resolution horizon, loaded before the entrance.

## Minor notes

- A stale "Nooo!" line stays up during a wrong answer (05-wrong-strike t1-3).
- The floor's warning tint snaps off within 2 frames (03-telegraph-strike t12-13).
- Eleven entrances are the same drop, and all deaths follow one template.
- The foreground heads float as black humps (10-player-defeat t1).
- Coins cover the score, which never rises (09-death t15-17).
- The Phantom's and Intestate's faces are dark holes at band size.

## Originality

No logo or brand.
- **Product Pusher:** see defect 6.
- **Longevity Lich:** a crowned chrome skeleton with green eyes, the "skeleton king" type (Lich King, Wraith King). Remake it with a new model; keep the hourglass.
- **Probate Tollgate:** a box on treads near WALL-E; its booth head keeps it clear.

The other nine are original.
