## 3. Major defects

1. **RANK UP! overprints the reward.** 08 t3: the banner lies across "18,450" and "The Fine Print falls"; its subtitle ("…u are now SENIOR ADVIS…") crosses the tile headers and is clipped at both screen edges. Fix: show it after the reward row settles, on its own dark plate, inside the safe area.
2. **The banked card blinks out.** 12 t1-3: in t2 the card is gone, an empty black panel above the old MISSED/GOT IT/EASY buttons; in t3 the next card is just there. Nothing is dealt from the deck. Fix: send the banked card into the vault and deal the next from the deck.
3. **Three toppers don't read.** 04-hub-4/5/6: the deck is an open black tray, the lectern a black kiosk, the book a black case, all dark on a dark hall. Fix: fanned white-faced cards, a lit open book on an angled lectern, a lit gold-tooled cover; a key light on each.
4. **The great book is a black hole.** 15-guide: an unlit black rectangle hangs dead centre over the rose window, the room's focal point. Fix: light its cover and page edges, turn or open it, and lower it so the glass frames it.
5. **One boss, two faces.** The Fine Print is a flat pixel figure with a monocle in 07 (row 4) and a white 3D robot with a slab hat in 09; every chapter-select portrait is 2D art. Fix: render the portraits from the same 3D busts.
6. **Earned cup looks locked.** 05: the earned centre cup is dark chrome, barely brighter than the two black locked cups. Fix: bright gold or silver with a lit star; locked ones as dim silhouettes.
7. **Two icon sets.** Flat pixel stars in 07, bronze 3D stars in 09; 09 tiles mix flat glyphs (crosshair, checkbox, XP arrow) with grey 3D renders too small to read. Fix: one set rendered from the models, at least 24 px.
8. **Grey-box prop in a hero shot.** 04-hub-4 (also 06b): an untextured navy block with yellow stripes and a post-mounted box beside the Memory Bank cabinet. Fix: a finished prop in the hall's chrome and neon, or remove it.
9. **Smeared chest on the examiner.** 13-exam: the centre chest panel is a noisy, low-resolution brown patch mid-screen. Fix: re-texture it to match the armour.

## 5. Originality

Nothing resembles a known character, robot, ship, vehicle, cabinet or logo: the cabinet is a generic upright, the striped sun is stock synthwave, and the skull-faced examiner is a trope, not a specific robot. Background machine names (Pixel Pilgrim, Strato Ace, Tin Tussle, Lantern Maze) are invented.
