# The final independent reviews

Each screen's last review, as it was written: a fresh agent that had never seen the project, given a contact
sheet and full-size frames of one screen from the final build, the house style to judge within, a description of
what moves, and this scale: 5 ship as is in a top commercial game, 4.5 one or two nits, 4 good with visible
weaknesses, 3 competent but plain ("be strict and do not round up"). Scores are in the order detail, lighting,
materials, depth, motion, cohesion, legibility. Frame numbers refer to the contact sheet each reviewer was given.
`PROGRESS.md` has the table of these scores, what was fixed after them and what was left.
