# Round P: the two reviews that came back

One more fix round was run on Boss Rush, the road (the title, with parts carried to Nest Egg Run), Risk Blaster and
the hub. Fresh frames of all five areas were captured from the round's build (commit `18b1466`), and each area was
to be reviewed once by a fresh agent with the same brief and scale as the earlier rounds. Two reviews had come back
when the owner closed the round, so Boss Rush, Risk Blaster and Nest Egg Run were not reviewed again.

The reviews are verbatim. Scores are in the order detail, lighting, materials, depth, motion, cohesion, legibility.
A sheet of the final frames for each of the five areas is beside this file: `round-p-title.jpg`, `round-p-hub.jpg`,
`round-p-blaster.jpg`, `round-p-racer.jpg`, `round-p-boss.jpg`.

WEAKNESSES:

## Arcade Hub

Frames 0 to 7 of `round-p-hub.jpg` (the review's F1 to F6 are frames 1 to 6).

WEAKNESSES:
