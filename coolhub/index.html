What both categories promise

Six things that are
true of every unit.

The products differ. The operating model does not — and the operating model is what makes a second and a third installation possible.

  • Easy installation

    No excavation, no poured concrete, no mechanical room. The unit bolts to a prepared pad and connects to a supply — so it is in the ground fast.

  • Short lead time

    Standard parts, standard surfaces, nothing bespoke to draw. Measured in weeks against the months a project-based build assumes.

  • No sales conversation required

    Specification lives on the page: dimensions, spray zones, flow rates and files. A self-serve path replaces the project-based sales cycle entirely.

  • Barely any service

    No certified operator and no scheduled maintenance visit. The unit is built to run through a season and be checked, not tended.

  • No technician needed

    Field-replaceable parts and simple mechanisms. A parks crew swaps a nozzle or a head with hand tools and the unit stays in service.

  • Water repurposing, easily

    Run-off feeds passive irrigation for nearby planting and amenities. A grade and an outlet — no added system complexity.
