Selling to us

Have you found
a wardrobe?

We buy outright, in cash, and we travel. Tailoring, outerwear, knitwear, leather goods and evening wear from roughly 1930 to 1990 are of most interest; so is anything with a maker’s ticket still in the pocket.

Send half a dozen photographs — the whole garment, the label, the inside of a cuff, and anything you think is damage — and we will tell you honestly whether it is something we can use. If it is, we will come to you.

Practicalities

Questions we get

Every listing carries measurements taken with the garment laid flat, in inches. The reliable method is to measure something you already own and wear happily, and compare. If you are between two of our numbers, write to us — after fifty years we have a fair sense of which cuts run generous.

Complimentary on orders over $300. A flat $18 otherwise. Everything is dispatched within two working days, steamed and wrapped in acid-free tissue, tracked and signed for. International shipping is quoted at cost before we take payment.

Fourteen days from delivery, unworn, with the tag still attached. We pay the return label within the UK. Because every piece is one of one, we cannot exchange — a return is a refund, and the garment goes back on the rail.

Yes. Our tailor works from the back room on Wednesdays and Fridays. Sleeves shortened, trousers tapered, waists taken in, hems raised. Ask for a quote before you buy and we will tell you what is possible in the seam allowance — and, occasionally, that it is not worth doing.

Yes. There is no back stock and no restock. When a listing sells it comes down, and the only way we would ever have the same garment again is if you sold it back to us — which, for the record, several people have.

For twenty-four hours, gladly, and longer if you are travelling to the shop to see it. Write or telephone. We would rather a coat went to someone who has looked at it properly.
