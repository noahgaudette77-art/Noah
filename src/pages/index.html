Since 1974

We have never
sold anything twice.

Every garment that passes through Marchmont Row is bought by hand, one at a time, from estate sales, house clearances and the occasional astonishing wardrobe. There is no second size, no restock, no next season.

What arrives is washed or dry-cleaned by a specialist, repaired where repair is honest, measured flat, photographed and described without flattery. If a seam has been restitched, we say so.

01

Bought by hand

No wholesale bales, no bulk sourcing. Two buyers, a van, and fifty years of knowing which houses cut a decent shoulder.

02

Described honestly

Condition notes name the flaws first. A worn cuff is a fact about a garment, not a secret to be discovered on arrival.

03

Measured flat

Vintage sizing is fiction. Every listing carries real measurements in inches, taken with the piece laid flat on the bench.

04

Repaired, not restored

We mend what threatens the garment and leave what tells its story. Patina is the reason you are here.
